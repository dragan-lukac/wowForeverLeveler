## Start of the game (beta, user)
- Every start camp has Junior gathering trainers. Skinning costs 10c, the Skinning Knife and the Mining Pick 10c each.
- Gathering bags (Skinning Satchel, Herb Pouch) cost 25c. No Mining bag is known.
- Each start zone has a quest that gives a free book for one gathering profession:
  Mulgore "A Humble Task" (level 1), Durotar "Galgar's Cactus Apple Surprise" (level 1),
  Tirisfal "Marla's Last Wish" (level 3).
- Junior trainers seen: Deathknell: Margaret Weaver (Skinning), Walter Mason (Mining), Florence Nightshade
  (Herbalism). Valley of Trials: Kagil (Skinning, The Den), Zor'la (Herbalism). Camp Narache: Nawka Wildsong (Skinning).
- No First Aid, Cooking or Fishing trainer in the first camp. They stand in Bloodhoof Village, Razor Hill and
  Brill (Gallows' End Tavern); Fishing at a lake (Brightwater Lake in Tirisfal).
- Apprentice Fishing costs 90c. The Basic Campfire Kit is a Cooking recipe at skill 20 (1s).
- Journeyman Cooking needs skill 50 (no level). Journeyman Fishing needs level 10.
- Sen'jin Village has an Enchanting trainer (Pa'zula) and an Alchemy trainer (Miao'zan).

## User's rules
- Learn Skinning the moment you have 20c; buy its bag at 25c. Take Herbalism or Mining from the free book.
- Learn Cooking and Fishing when the money is there. Every character gathers at the start.
- Keep Ruined Leather Scraps (sell only when desperate for money).
- Levels 1-6: no bag materials, except the 25c gathering bag.

## Professions
- Healing potions are First Aid in Forever (game scan): Minor Healing Potion at skill 1 (Peacebloom), Lesser 55
  (Briarthorn), Healing 110 (Bruiseweed). Alchemy makes elixirs, mana potions and "Discolored" healing potions.
- Enchanting wands (game scan): Novice's Practice Wand skill 1 (1 Simple Wood, 1 Mote of Magic; binds when
  equipped), Lesser Magic Wand 10, Greater Magic Wand 70, Lesser Mystic 155, Greater Mystic 175. Character levels: check on live.
- Mote of Magic: trainer vendors sell it for levels 1-20; 1 Strange Dust gives 3 Motes.
- Linen Bag needs Tailoring 5. Many recipes turn grey about 25 points earlier than in Classic.
- Disenchanting armor gives mostly dust, weapons mostly essence (user's test).
- One camp object per player per hour. Camp buffs last 1 hour.
