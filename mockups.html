Planner mockup

One addon window, as in game, with real data. Pick who is logged in: the window shows that character's tabs. Hover a line for details; drag the window's corner to resize; A- / A+ change the font.

How it fits together

Tabs by character

WhoTabs
Every leveling characterNext · Dungeons · Professions · Team
Bank (bank)Next · Market · Team

Next is always first: for each area the step now and the one after. It moves on by itself as you finish steps.

What each tab is for

  • Dungeons: the next dungeon stops, the entrance with an arrow, and every quest chain: start, next quests, dungeon quest, with NPC and spot. No zone quests: Polestar does those.
  • Professions: per profession what to make now and next, and the materials of the current skill step.
  • Team: who to play next by rested XP, and the team's materials for every crafter's step.
  • Market (Bank only): sell, buy cheap, craft to sell, hold.

Where the data comes from

  1. Each character counts its bags and bank at every login, and records level, quests, professions and rested XP.
  2. Everyone mails materials to Bank. She sees what the whole team holds.
  3. Auctionator scan on Bank gives today's prices; the usual price is the middle of earlier scans.
  4. The plan (quests, chains, dungeons, recipes, material totals) is built on the computer by Sync and travels with the addon.

A day with the team

  1. Log in the character the Team step says (most rested XP). Follow Polestar for quests, Next for dungeons and crafting.
  2. Before logging out: mail materials to Bank, log out in an inn.
  3. Bank: collect mail, scan the AH, do Next: sell, buy cheap, send crafters their step's materials.
