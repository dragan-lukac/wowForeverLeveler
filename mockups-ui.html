Professions tab: two ways to draw it

Same character, same data. Left is A, cards; right is B, compact list. Both use only what the addon can draw in the game: flat colours, game icons, bars. Tell me what you like from each, for example "the card headers from A, the material rows from B".

A. Cards

Each profession is a box with a dark header: icon, name, skill bar. Steps are numbered, with the cost on the right and an arrow button. Market and "temporary" notes are coloured boxes. Materials are small tiles with bars.
